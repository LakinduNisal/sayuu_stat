Industrial Statistics & Mathematical Finance

University of Colombo - Syllabus & Academic Pathway Analysis

This interactive application analyzes the comprehensive structure of the B.Sc. degree program. It explores how foundational mathematics and statistics evolve into highly specialized tracks: Industrial Statistics (IS), focusing on quality, process optimization, and operations, and Mathematical Finance (MF), focusing on stochastic modeling, risk management, and quantitative trading.

Degree Composition & Credit Weighting

This section provides a macro-level view of the four-year degree structure. The visualization below breaks down the total academic credits into core disciplines. It highlights the heavy emphasis on foundational quantitative skills before branching into applied domains. Interact with the chart to see specific credit allocations.

📊

Foundational Years (1 & 2)

Students build a rigorous base in pure mathematics, probability theory, statistical inference, and computer programming. This common core is mandatory for all students before specialization.

🛤️

Specialization Years (3 & 4)

Students diverge into either IS or MF streams. The curriculum shifts from general theory to highly applied, domain-specific models, practical projects, and a final year research dissertation.

Interactive Curriculum Explorer

Navigate through the academic journey. Select an academic year to view the modules. For Years 3 and 4, toggle between the Industrial Statistics and Mathematical Finance streams to compare the divergent specialized subject matter. Click on any module card for detailed syllabus information.

Competency & Skills Matrix

This section illustrates the distinct professional profiles developed by the end of the degree. The radar chart compares the core competencies gained by a graduate of the Industrial Statistics stream versus the Mathematical Finance stream, highlighting their respective strengths in the job market.

IS Graduate Profile

  • • High proficiency in experimental design & quality assurance.
  • • Strong operational research and optimization skills.
  • • Target Careers: Quality Engineer, Data Scientist, Operations Analyst.

MF Graduate Profile

  • • Advanced knowledge of stochastic calculus and pricing models.
  • • Expertise in financial risk assessment and time-series forecasting.
  • • Target Careers: Quantitative Analyst, Risk Manager, Actuary.
